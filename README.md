# Aimshot

A camera app that shows you where to aim before you take the photo.

## v0.1
- **Scan**: finds the interesting part of the scene and draws a 4:5 frame around it (rule of thirds).
- **Draw your own**: drag on the screen to draw the frame you want.
- **Guide**: move the phone until the dot sits in the ring. The shutter glows yellow and the phone taps when you're lined up.
- **Shoot**: the photo is cropped to the frame and saved to Photos.

## How to get the app on your phone
1. Every push to `main` builds the app automatically (Actions tab).
2. Open the latest green run, scroll to **Artifacts**, download **Aimshot-ipa**.
3. Unzip it to get `Aimshot.ipa`, then sideload it as usual.

No Mac or Xcode needed: the project is generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml` and built on GitHub's cloud Mac.
